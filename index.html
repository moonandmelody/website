What is Moon & Melody?

It is a gourmet home kitchen created around the simple pleasure of slowing down — stepping away from the everyday rush and making space for the moments that matter.

From indulgent sipping chocolates and fragrant teas to beautifully crafted coffees and little bites, everything is made to turn an ordinary evening into a moment worth savouring - whether it's time spent with yourself or with the people you love.

Because sometimes, all we need is a little indulgence, a little presence, and a little faith.

What Makes Us Different?

It's the little things.

  • Small-batch recipes

  • Thoughtful pairings

  • Ingredients chosen with intention

  • A menu designed for lingering

  • And a little message from the universe, waiting inside every order

At Moon & Melody, we don't just want to make something delicious.

We want to make the moment around it feel special!

Our Story

Moon & Melody began with a craving.

As two people who love a good cup of hot chocolate, we kept searching for one in Bengaluru that felt truly indulgent — rich, silky, made with good chocolate, and worth slowing down for. We kept looking, exploring, and eventually, we decided to make it ourselves.

What started in our kitchen with couverture chocolate became Midnight Luxe, our signature dark hot chocolate, alongside Moon Dance - a white chocolate variation. But we didn't want tea and coffee lovers to feel left out, so the menu grew into a little collection of indulgent drinks and comforting bites — all made with the warmth of a home kitchen, but with the quality and thoughtfulness of something special.

Because that's what we wanted Moon & Melody to feel like: homemade, but elevated. Indulgent, but familiar.

But somewhere along the way, we realised we were creating something bigger than food.

We both remember the little thrill of childhood — finding a collectible tucked inside a chips packet, a bubblegum wrapper, or some ordinary little treat. Those tiny surprises made everyday moments feel magical. Somewhere between growing up, rushing through work and constantly being on the move, we felt like we had lost that joy.

So we decided to bring a little of it back.

Every Moon & Melody order carries a lunar cycle collectible card — a small message from the universe, meant to find you wherever you are in life. Not an answer, not a prediction. Just a little reminder to pause, have faith, and trust your journey. Because we believe slowing down is a luxury of its own.

An evening doesn't always need to be productive. Sometimes it can be a warm cup of chocolate shared with someone you love. A long conversation with your sibling. A date that lasts a little longer. A quiet evening with yourself. Or simply a moment to savour something delicious.

Moon & Melody was created for those moments.

For slowing down. For being present. For finding joy in the little things again.

Founders

Shravya

Melody

The Founder, The Chef & The Creative Mind

Moon & Melody is, in many ways, an extension of Shravya's love for beautiful things, good food and thoughtful details.

From carefully choosing the brand colours and shaping the visual identity to developing the menu, she has poured a little bit of herself into every corner of Moon & Melody. She even taught herself to bake because she wanted to be able to create and serve the things she genuinely loves — and give people the best version of them she could.

She is the hands behind the food, the eye behind the aesthetics, and the creative force that keeps asking, “How can we make this a little more special?”

The Co-Founder, The Investor & The Tech Genius

Every creative idea needs someone willing to say, “Okay, let's make it happen.” That's Shashank.

The investor behind the project, the person cheering from the front row, and the one who somehow turns Shravya's “I have an idea…” into something that actually exists.
